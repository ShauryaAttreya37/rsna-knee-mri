# Training Reports

This directory receives only identifier-free aggregate training summaries.
The first accepted artifact is `gold_overfit_summary.json`, generated by
`scripts/10_gold_overfit.py` after the same-cohort gold-label memorization test.

Before launching that run, `scripts/training_preflight.py --device cuda` must
pass. Environment creation is reproducible through
`scripts/setup_training_env.sh` and `requirements-training.txt`.

Do not commit study/series identifiers, cached tensors, cache mappings, model
checkpoints, per-study predictions, raw reports, or DICOM-derived images. Those
artifacts remain under `/home/shaurya/rsna-data/` on the training VM.

Overfit metrics are pipeline sanity checks only. They must never be described
as held-out validation performance or evidence of generalization.

The real imaging path is driven interactively from
`notebooks/04_imaging_training.py` through Jupytext. Its weak-pretraining
partition contains only the 4,349 non-gold studies; all 58 gold studies are
excluded so their images cannot leak into cross-validation. Each fine-tuning
run trains on four gold folds and evaluates on the untouched fifth fold.
Long-running cells are disabled by default and must be enabled explicitly.

Resumable checkpoints are written under
`/home/shaurya/rsna-data/models/imaging_v1/`. Only the identifier-free stage
summaries produced under this directory belong in Git.

## DINOv2 ViT-S/14 five-fold baseline

The leak-free weak-pretraining and five-fold gold fine-tuning sequence completed
on 2026-09-05. The best held-out macro AUC values for folds 0 through 4 were
`0.7085`, `0.6929`, `0.6768`, `0.7866`, and `0.7546`. Their unweighted mean is
`0.7239`, with a sample standard deviation of `0.0455`.

The fold aggregate is recorded in
`dinov2_vits14_cross_validation_summary.json`. Pooled OOF evaluation is complete
and recorded in `dinov2_vits14_oof_summary.json`. Raw pooled macro AUC is
`0.6863`; Brier score is `0.1969`, and log loss is `0.6015`. Label-free
per-fold logit standardization raises pooled AUC to `0.7173`, which diagnoses
large checkpoint score-scale drift but is not a deployable test-time method.

Mean per-target AUC is strongest for Medial OA (`0.8843`), Effusion (`0.8467`),
Synovitis (`0.8343`), and Baker's cyst (`0.7985`). Contusion (`0.5345`) and
Fracture (`0.5181`) remain near chance and are the first targets for controlled
error analysis. The five best checkpoints remain external under
`/home/shaurya/rsna-data/models/imaging_v1/gold_fold_{0..4}/best.pt`.

The next gate is a valid five-checkpoint Kaggle submission. Do not repeat this
training recipe. Later experiments must first show a credible gain on fixed
folds and must target label quality, anatomical geometry, or weak targets.

## Imaging v2: three-source public weak labels

The controlled weak-supervision replacement completed across all five folds on
2026-09-05 with architecture, cache, fold assignments, and fine-tuning settings
held fixed. Fold-best macro AUC values are `0.8754`, `0.7982`, `0.7355`,
`0.8497`, and `0.8034`; every fold improved over imaging v1. The five-fold mean
rose from `0.7239` to `0.8125`.

The strict 58-study pooled result is recorded in
`imaging_v2_public_labels_oof_summary.json`: macro AUC `0.7835`, Brier score
`0.1603`, and log loss `0.4977`. This is a `+0.0973` pooled AUC gain over v1,
but it is not evidence of a top-ten leaderboard score. The five best
checkpoints remain external under
`/home/shaurya/rsna-data/models/imaging_v2_public_labels/gold_fold_{0..4}/best.pt`.
